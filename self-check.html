A five-question self-check

Where does your organization actually stand?

Five questions, answered honestly, usually reveal more than a strategy offsite. Answer for how decisions actually get made today, not how the org chart says they should.

Do decisions across your organization reflect your strategy?

Can you tell which decisions your management or employees are currently working on?

Are the strategic and tactical decisions elaborated and documented according to your expectations?

Do you know which decisions need to be reevaluated, and can you demonstrate governance over them?

When a manager leaves, does the reasoning behind their decisions stay in the company?

Before you decide: the ten-item pass

Run the ten-item pass over the decision on your desk.

0 / 10
  • Have I framed the right question, one with more than two answers?
  • Is this environment complicated or complex? Analyze, or probe?
  • Which tier is this: strategic, tactical, or operational?
  • Have I written down the assumptions, each with a review date?
  • Are there genuine alternatives, not one real option and two straw men?
  • Is the do-nothing option priced, its costs and risks made explicit?
  • Are the criteria weighted and set before gathering evidence?
  • Is there one named owner accountable for the outcome?
  • Will the reasoning travel, not just the conclusion, to those who execute?
  • Is there a review date: a moment the decision must justify itself again?
Tap each item as you clear it.
