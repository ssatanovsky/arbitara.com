Why, and on what basis

To respond to your message and, if you say "Get updates," to contact you when Arbitara opens more broadly. The legal basis is your consent, given by submitting the form.

Where it's stored

Submissions go to a Cloudflare Worker and are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV — never in this site's source code or its public GitHub repository. The public website itself is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which does not receive or store form data.

Who else sees it

Cloudflare, as our infrastructure processor, and no one else. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies. Two browser-local values are used, neither shared with any server or third party:

  • Theme preference (light/dark) — localStorage, persists until you clear it.
  • Site password unlock, only while password protection is enabled — sessionStorage, cleared when you close the browser.
